# 2026-10-06 — P2.9 Hardening

Part of #1193 (phase P2 — one design language, one shell). The phase's
hardening block is five bullets; this PR's first job was to establish which of
them P2.1–P2.5 had already satisfied, because re-implementing a control that
exists produces a second, divergent oracle rather than more assurance.

Three of the five already had a mechanism. One of those three was measuring
the wrong palette.

## Bullet 1 — contrast. What P2.3 ranged over, and three things it got wrong

`tests/guards/token-contrast-wcag.test.ts` shipped with P2.3 and did real
relative-luminance maths with alpha compositing on the text colour. Its
docblock named its own population honestly: "every `--content-*` tone against
that theme's `--bg-default` and `--bg-page`… the two grounds body text
actually sits on". Measured against the codebase, those are not the two:

| surface | `bg-*` utility uses in `src/` | in the P2.3 gate |
|---|---|---|
| `bg-muted` | 204 | no |
| `bg-default` | 142 | yes |
| `bg-subtle` | 81 | no |
| `bg-elevated` | 52 | no |
| `bg-page` | 32 | yes |

`bg-muted` is the most-used surface in the application and was outside the
gate. 337 of 511 neutral-surface utility uses were ungated.

**1. The ground list is now DERIVED, not named.** Every `bg-*` token that is
not the inverted surface, the modal scrim, or one of the ten status surfaces.
That yields the five above today and absorbs a new neutral surface the day it
is declared. The explicit membership assertion is what holds it (see
*Decisions*).

**2. A translucent ground was measured raw.** `--bg-subtle` is
`rgba(139, 130, 118, 0.07)` in light and `rgba(21, 94, 72, 0.30)` in dark; it
renders as whatever is underneath it, tinted. Alpha on the TEXT was already
composited; alpha on the GROUND was not, and it is the larger of the two
effects. Each translucent surface is now flattened onto every opaque surface
it can sit on (`bg-page`, `bg-default`) and the WORST result gates. Getting
this wrong in either direction is costly: measuring `bg-subtle` raw reports
`--content-subtle` in light at **1.41:1**, a phantom failure, and measuring
the five status tints raw reports all 15 tone-on-tint pairs failing when none
does.

**3. «Слънце» did not inherit from dark, and both P2.3 artefacts thought it
did.** `attributesFor('sunlight')` sets `data-theme="light"` AND
`data-contrast="high"` on the same element. Both are single attribute
selectors, so specificity ties and source order decides: `[data-contrast="high"]`
(tokens.css:1106) wins for the 25 tokens it declares and
`[data-theme="light"]` (tokens.css:733) supplies the remaining 105. The guard
and `scripts/generate-tokens.mjs` both did `spec.themes[theme][name] ??
spec.themes.dark[name]`.

**82 of 130 tokens resolve differently** under the two models. For the guard
that was latent — the ten body tones and the two grounds it happened to
measure are all declared in the high-contrast block, so no cell was wrong
*yet*, and widening the ground list is what would have made it wrong. For the
generator it had already shipped: Swift has no cascade, so every theme is
emitted fully resolved, and **58 colour arms** of `AgrentTheme.highContrast`
carried the dark forest greens and golds while the web «Слънце» showed the
cream light palette. P2.8 is the consumer.

### What is gated now

```
body tones (10) × neutral ramp (5) × themes (3)        = 150, +20 flattening grounds = 170
status tone on its own tint (5) × themes (3)           =  15, +15 flattening grounds =  30
content-inverted × solid surfaces (5) × themes (3)     =                               15
                                                                                   ──────
                                                                                      215
```

`content-inverted` is the third of those and is new coverage, not a widening.
P2.3's docblock said it was "excluded BY NAME and asserted separately"; there
was no separate contrast assertion anywhere in the repo, so a tone with **36
call sites** was measured by nothing. It is now measured on the five surfaces
it actually rides (`bg-inverted` plus the four `bg-*-emphasis` chips). The
solid list is derived too, because the status family is asymmetric — five
tints, four solid chips, `attention` has no `-emphasis`. Hardcoding five is
how the first version of this gate failed.

### The three tokens that genuinely failed AA

Fixed in `design/tokens.json` and regenerated. No threshold was relaxed and
nothing was exempted.

| token | was | now | binding pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| `light.content-subtle` | `#6B6B6B` | `#666666` | 4.44:1 → **4.79:1** on `bg-subtle` flattened over `bg-page` |
| `dark.content-info` | `#60a5fa` | `#7DB6FC` | 4.48:1 → **5.41:1** on `bg-elevated` (52 uses) |
| `dark.bg-info-emphasis` | `#2563EB` | `#3B82F6` | `content-inverted` 3.44:1 → **4.83:1** |

The first is the GAP-CI-77 tier again: its own comment claimed "4.9:1 on
bg-default", which was true, and is why nobody noticed the selection surface.
The third has zero call sites today — but `content-inverted`'s own token
comment says it is the ink for "inverted (gold) + solid-status surfaces", the
other three solid-status surfaces already cleared AA, and changing a token
nothing renders carries no visual risk.

### The one exclusion, and why it is not an exemption

The social surfaces `bubble-own` / `bubble-other` (P2.2) are body-text grounds
and belong in the gate. Two pairs fail: `content-subtle` on `bubble-own` is
**4.34:1** in dark and `content-link` on `bubble-own` is **4.45:1** in light,
both because P2.2 pointed `bubble-own` at the 16%-gold `--brand-subtle` rather
than mint a colour — deliberately, so that "inventing six new colours here
would be six more things for P2.3's contrast test to have to justify".

They have **zero call sites in `src/`** (measured: 0 uses of any
`bg|text|border|fill|stroke|ring-bubble-*` utility). Satisfying them means
moving `--brand-default`, the brand P2.4 had just chosen, to suit a surface
nothing renders. So the exclusion is CONDITIONAL ON THE ABSENCE and the
absence is asserted: the guard walks `src/`, floors the file count, and fails
the moment a bubble utility appears — naming the file, the two failing ratios
and what to do. `presence-*` and `unread` are out of scope on their merits:
dots and badges are non-text graphics (1.4.11 at 3:1).

## Bullet 2 — the mobile axe check did not exist

`tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts` has 7 surfaces and the right severity gate —
`serious` + `critical` must be empty, `moderate` / `minor` logged. It carries
no `@mobile` tag, and `playwright.config.ts` gives `mobile-android` and
`mobile-iphone` `grep: /@mobile/`. So **0 of 7 scans had ever run at a phone
viewport**; all 7 ran on `chromium` at 1280×720.

The jsdom sweep is not a substitute. `tests/rendered/ag-pages-a11y.test.tsx`
does run at a phone viewport — 6 surfaces × 2 viewports, and `jest-axe`'s
`toHaveNoViolations()` is stricter than this gate, failing on every impact —
but jsdom computes no layout and loads no stylesheet, so axe's `color-contrast`
and target-size rules cannot evaluate there at all. Those are exactly the
mobile-specific rules. Across the repo: 15 `axe()` calls in 5 rendered files,
12 of 21 scans at a phone viewport, 0 able to judge a colour.

Tagging `a11y.spec.ts` `@mobile` would have been the wrong fix — the desktop
project's `grepInvert: /@mobile/` would then have excluded it, trading one
blind viewport for the other. Instead:

- `runA11yScan` moved to `tests/e2e/a11y-scan.ts`, shared. Copying it would
  have forked an oracle whose settle logic is the accumulated answer to four
  flake classes (#266 and successors).
- `tests/e2e/mobile/a11y-mobile.spec.ts`, tagged `@mobile`, scans the DOM that
  only exists below `md:` — the `md:hidden` bottom-tab bar (P2.5), the
  `MobileCardList` branch of `<DataTable mobileFallback="card">`, and the
  filter toolbar as an OPEN vaul bottom sheet (a dialog's focus trap and
  labelling are what an axe scan is for). 5 scans × 2 phone projects = **10**.
- The last of the five forces «Слънце». It is the only RUNTIME check of that
  theme in the repo — the token guard is static, and cannot see a hardcoded
  colour, an inline style, or a token used against a ground the static pairing
  does not model.

`playwright test --list` confirms the split: 19 tests in 2 files, 7 on
`chromium` and 6 on each phone project, with no overlap.

## Bullet 3 — the visual set is NOT delivered

Measured, not assumed:

- `toHaveScreenshot` / `toMatchSnapshot` call sites under `tests/e2e/`: **0**.
- Visual-regression services in `package.json` or any workflow (Percy,
  Chromatic, reg-suit, BackstopJS): **0**.
- The one spec named `*-visual*`, `tests/e2e/ag-map-visual.spec.ts`,
  deliberately refuses pixel snapshots with a reason that still holds: "the
  maplibre WebGL canvas is non-deterministic across GPUs / CI runners, so a
  pixel snapshot of it would be perpetually flaky". It asserts deterministic
  STRUCTURE and attaches a screenshot to the report as a human artifact with
  no diff assertion.

So there is no mechanism, and the repo has an explicit, reasoned precedent
against introducing one for a WebGL surface. Standing up 60 baselines
(20 screens × 3 themes) under this phase would mean: committing 60 PNGs that
differ between a developer's machine and the runner on font rasterisation
alone; 60 more per phone project if the set is meant to be mobile; and
threading them through a serial suite with a 25-minute step budget whose
slowest healthy shard is ~6 minutes of Playwright — each screenshot needs a
login, a navigation, a theme switch and a settle. The predictable outcome is a
permanently-red or permanently-`--update-snapshots` gate, which is worse than
no gate because it trains people to regenerate rather than look.

What it would actually take, so the decision is costed rather than deferred:

1. A committed, agreed list of the 20 screens. The phrase "most-used" has no
   source today — there is no analytics pipeline in this repo that ranks
   screens by traffic, so the list is somebody's judgement and should be
   written down as such before 60 artefacts are pinned to it.
2. A deterministic rendering environment. In practice a container image with
   pinned fonts used by BOTH local and CI runs, or a hosted service that
   rasterises centrally (Percy/Chromatic), which is a procurement decision.
3. A masking convention for the genuinely non-deterministic regions — the
   maplibre canvas, relative timestamps («преди 5 мин» from P2.1), seeded ids.
4. Its own CI job, off the `e2e-shard` critical path, because a 60-screenshot
   sweep does not fit the existing budget.
5. A review gesture: somebody has to LOOK. A pixel diff that only ever gets
   `--update-snapshots` is an artifact nothing checks.

Item 1 is cheap and worth doing regardless; the device peer's sunlight test
(bullet 4) needs the same list. Items 2–5 are a PR of their own.

## Bullet 5 — rollback already was a revert of `tokens.json`

Verified rather than trusted:

- `src/styles/tokens.css` holds **276** custom-property declarations;
  `design/tokens.json` holds **273** tokens (130 dark + 118 light + 25
  highContrast) and the generator rewrites exactly 273 of those lines. The
  other **3** are the `--duration-*` reduced-motion overrides inside
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, which `themeOfLine()` skips by
  tracking media depth — hand-authored durations, no colour among them.
- `src/app/globals.css` declares 21 custom properties and every one is a
  `var(--token)` alias with no value of its own, so each re-themes through the
  token it points at and holds nothing to revert.
- `npm run tokens:check` runs in CI (`ci.yml`, the `Lint` job) and
  `tests/guards/tokens-generated-in-sync.test.ts` executes the real generator
  with `--check`.

The hole was forward-looking: nothing stopped a 179th colour being declared in
another stylesheet, where the generator never reads and `--check` never
compares. `tokens-generated-in-sync.test.ts` now walks every `.css` under
`src/`, strips comments, and fails on any colour-literal custom-property
declaration outside `tokens.css` — with a positive control (≥150 found INSIDE
it, measured 178) so an empty offender list cannot mean "the regex matched
nothing".

## Decisions

- **The derived floor slides with the population, so it is not the teeth.**
  `gate()` floors each check at `themes × grounds × tones`, which catches a
  tone or ground that stops RESOLVING. It does not catch the population
  shrinking, because the floor is computed from `ramp.length`: narrowing the
  ramp back to P2.3's two grounds gives 60 pairs against a floor of 60, and
  passes. Measured, not reasoned. The teeth against a shrinking population are
  the explicit membership assertions in the inputs test, which is what
  reddened. Both are kept and the guard says which does what.
- **The flattening control asserts a RATIO, not a threshold.** The first
  version asserted `flattened > 4.5`, which restates the gate — so reverting a
  token value failed the control too and the two signals stopped being
  separable. It now asserts that flattening moves the answer by more than
  double, which is the property the control owns.
- **The guard cross-checks its cascade against the generator's OUTPUT, not
  against a copy of the constant.** Two copies of a chain can agree with each
  other while both disagree with what shipped — which is exactly the state
  P2.3 left. The guard asserts the banner `Tokens.swift` emits names the chain
  it uses, and parses three `.highContrast` colour arms back out and compares
  them to its own resolution. Breaking the generator's `CASCADE` and
  regenerating reddens it.
- **`tokens.css` is still hand-authored prose with generated values.** Not
  revisited: 30 guard files read that file directly, and `r13-secondary-brand-tokens`
  requires a rationale to sit BETWEEN two specific declarations. The generator
  rewrites values in place and the three token fixes here touched 6 lines.
- **The scan helper's theme force reads itself back.** `assertThemeApplied`
  runs after the force and before `analyze()`. Without it a «Слънце» label
  over a dark document is green for the wrong reason — the same class as
  writing `data-theme="sunlight"`, which selects no palette at all.
- **The mobile spec's first test is a control, and it is the load-bearing
  one.** Every other test asserts an ABSENCE of serious findings, which a page
  rendering the desktop shell — or nothing at all — satisfies perfectly. The
  control asserts the viewport is under 768px AND that the `md:hidden`
  bottom-tab bar actually mounted.
- **Nothing was added for bullet 3.** 60 screenshots nothing compares against
  is not a control, and the repo's own `ag-map-visual.spec.ts` already decided
  that question for its hardest surface.
